General

How do I start investing? expand_more
To begin, complete your profile verification by uploading your ID, fund your account via bank transfer or credit card, and select an investment plan that fits your risk profile. Our automated onboarding will guide you through every step.
How are returns calculated? expand_more
Returns are calculated daily based on the net asset value (NAV) of your portfolio components. We use Time-Weighted Return (TWR) to show you how your investments are performing, excluding the effects of deposits and withdrawals.
shield

Account Security

Is my data secure? expand_more
Yes, your security is our top priority. We use AES-256 bank-grade encryption for all sensitive data and mandate Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for all account activities. Your funds are kept in segregated accounts at regulated custodian banks.
What happens if I lose my phone? expand_more
If you lose access to your 2FA device, you can use the recovery codes provided during setup. Alternatively, you can contact our security team for a manual identity verification process to restore access.
payments

Deposits & Withdrawals

What are the minimum deposits? expand_more
Our minimum initial deposit is $500 for the Basic plan. For Premium and Elite tiers, minimums are $10,000 and $50,000 respectively, offering lower management fees and dedicated account managers.
How long do withdrawals take? expand_more
Standard withdrawals are processed within 1-3 business days. Depending on your bank's processing times, funds usually appear in your account within 5 business days of the request.
